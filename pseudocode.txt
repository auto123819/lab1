Lab 1 · Part D · Pseudocode
Write an algorithm for ONE of these, as numbered steps, the way we wrote the phone-book algorithm in class:
  (a) find the largest number in a stack of exam papers, one paper at a time;
  (b) make a cup of tea for a robot that knows nothing;
  (c) guess a number between 1 and 100 that a friend is thinking of, using yes/no questions.
Rules: at least 6 numbered lines; use at least one "if" and one loop ("go back to line N", "repeat" or "while").
Replace the example below with your own.

1  Set the lower bound to 1 and the top bound to 100
2  Calculate the average middle of the number between the lower and the top number
3  Ask that is it the middle number 
4  if the friend say "Yes", stop.
5  else ask that is it bigger than the middle number
6  if the friend say "Yes", add +1 to the middle number and set it to lower number instead of the middle number
7  if the friend say "No", add -1 to the middle number and set it to top number instead of the middle number
8  Go back to line 2
